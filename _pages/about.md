I work on AI for photonics and agentic chip design. As an NSF Graduate Research Fellow in [Di Liang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/di-liang-7728255/)’s [Large Scale Integrated Photonics Lab](https://lsip.engin.umich.edu/), I use inverse design to shape photonic devices and develop AI agents that help turn circuit specifications into chip layouts.

Before Michigan, I earned my B.S. in electrical engineering and computer science (EECS) and materials science and engineering (MSE) from UC Berkeley.
