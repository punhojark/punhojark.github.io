I am a Ph.D. student in **Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan**, working on silicon and integrated photonics in the [Large Scale Integrated Photonics Lab](https://lsip.engin.umich.edu/).

My research brings together **machine learning and photonic device design**. I develop inverse-design methods that make compact optical devices more interpretable, efficient to optimize, and ready for fabrication.

Previously, I studied Electrical Engineering & Computer Science and Materials Science & Engineering at **UC Berkeley**, graduating with EECS Honors. I have also worked at Apple, Amazon Web Services, and STMicroelectronics.
