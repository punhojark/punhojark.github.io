Junho Park

Ph.D. student at the University of Michigan

I work on AI for photonics and agentic chip design. As an NSF Graduate Research Fellow in Di Liang’s Large Scale Integrated Photonics Lab, I use inverse design to shape photonic devices and develop AI agents that help turn circuit specifications into chip layouts.

Before Michigan, I earned my B.S. in electrical engineering and computer science (EECS) and materials science and engineering (MSE) from UC Berkeley.
